Bun Cha - Vietnamese Meatballs

Vietnamese pork meatballs with noodles and a light dipping sauce.

Bun Cha - Vietnamese Meatballs

Meatballs:

  • 250 g pork mince (ground pork)(Note 1)
  • 1 tbsp fish sauce (Note 2)

Nuoc Cham (Sauce):

  • 3 tbsp white sugar
  • 2 tbsp lime juice

Serving:

  • 100 g vermicelli noodles , dried
  • Sauce: Mix ingredients. Set aside 10 minutes+.

Meatballs:

  • Mix all ingredients.
  • Shape into 6 patties.

Assemble Bowls:

  • Place noodles in bowl.
1. Pork: Can use chicken or turkey.
2. Nuoc Cham: Not as strong as others. 

3. Adapted from a few cookbooks.
